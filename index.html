Privacy settings

This website uses GoatCounter for visitor statistics. The following analytics data is collected:

  • Sessions: pageviews from the same visitor are grouped into a session for up to 8 hours.
  • Country: the visitor’s country.
  • Region: the visitor’s region, where available. The level of detail varies by country.
  • User-Agent: the browser and operating system are derived from the User-Agent header. The header itself is not stored.
  • Language: the visitor’s preferred language is derived from the language information sent by the browser. Only the language is stored, not the original header.

GoatCounter is designed to provide privacy-friendly, cookie-free website analytics. You can opt out of analytics for this browser below. Your choice is saved in this browser.

For more information, see GoatCounter’s privacy information .
